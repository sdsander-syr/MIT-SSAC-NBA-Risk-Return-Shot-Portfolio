The submitted SSAC27 abstract and the two graphics it includes.

  ssac_abstract.tex   source; compile with pdflatex, run twice
  ssac_abstract.pdf   compiled, 2 pages
  arbitrage (5).pdf   Figure 2 graphic
  cf3d (1).pdf        Figure 1 graphic

The filenames with spaces and parentheses are what \includegraphics in the .tex
expects; keep them as they are.

To regenerate the graphics from data instead of using these copies, run
reproduce.py then cf3d.py and arb_fig.py from the repository root. They write to
figures/ under the same names.
